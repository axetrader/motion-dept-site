Privacy Policy

Effective: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Motion Dept ("Motion Dept," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use our website, connect a supported social account, or use our content, publishing, commerce, analytics, or related services.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

We may collect account details, contact information, content and media you upload or create, captions, product or affiliate information, disclosure selections, publishing instructions, support messages, and other information you choose to provide.

Information from TikTok

When you choose to connect TikTok, Motion Dept only requests permissions that are needed for enabled features. The planned TikTok integration uses Login Kit basic identity access (user.info.basic) and Content Posting permission (video.publish) when approved and authorized.

Depending on the approved TikTok integration and your authorization, TikTok may provide an app-specific user identifier, display name, avatar, granted scopes, creator information used for publishing, privacy and interaction settings, post or publish identifiers, and status information. OAuth access and refresh tokens are treated as credentials and are intended to be stored server-side.

Technical and operational information

We may process security logs, timestamps, request identifiers, device/browser information, service errors, audit records, quota or rate-limit information, and other technical data needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and verify the service.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and secure Motion Dept; authenticate connected accounts; prepare creator-approved content; honor selected publishing settings; submit authorized posts; report publishing status; provide analytics; measure costs, revenue signals, and content performance; prevent abuse; comply with provider rules; respond to support requests; and improve service reliability.

We do not use a TikTok authorization to create unrelated objectives, expand permissions on our own, or publish content without the user action and approvals required by the service.

3. TikTok publishing and user control

Before a TikTok publishing action is sent, Motion Dept is designed to show the connected creator account and the content being prepared for posting, use creator information returned by TikTok, honor available privacy and interaction settings, and require express user consent before upload or publication.

You may deny requested TikTok scopes, disconnect TikTok, or revoke Motion Dept's TikTok authorization. Revocation stops future authorized access, subject to processing needed to complete a request already lawfully underway and any retention required by law or security obligations.

4. Commercial and affiliate content

Motion Dept may support content concerning products, affiliate offers, a user's own business, or third-party brands. We use disclosure selections and related metadata to help users follow applicable platform and advertising requirements. We do not represent that using Motion Dept automatically satisfies every legal, advertising, endorsement, or platform obligation.

5. How we share information

We may share information with social platforms you direct us to use, infrastructure and security service providers acting for us, analytics or operational vendors needed to run the service, and authorities or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or the integrity of the service.

We do not sell TikTok user data. We do not share OAuth access or refresh tokens with advertisers or other users.

6. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including account operation, authorized publishing, analytics, financial records, security, dispute handling, and legal or provider requirements. Different records may have different retention periods. Credentials and provider data are subject to provider-specific retention and deletion rules where applicable.

When information is no longer needed, we delete, de-identify, or otherwise dispose of it in accordance with applicable requirements and our retention controls.

7. Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements. You may also request deletion of TikTok-sourced data associated with your Motion Dept account and may disconnect or revoke TikTok authorization.

To make a privacy or deletion request, use the contact method on our Support page. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, including data portability or rights concerning certain sharing, sale, or targeted advertising practices.

8. Security

Motion Dept uses technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect credentials, account data, and service records. Sensitive provider credentials are intended to remain server-side and outside public source code. No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Cookies and local storage

Motion Dept may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies for authentication, session integrity, security, preferences, and service operation. If we later introduce non-essential advertising or tracking technologies, we will update this Policy and provide notices or choices where required.

10. Third-party services

TikTok and other connected services are independent third parties with their own terms and privacy practices. Your use of those services remains subject to their policies. Motion Dept is not TikTok and is not endorsed by TikTok.

11. Age restriction

Motion Dept is intended for users who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly offer the service to children under 13 or knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service, provider integrations, or legal requirements change. We will post the updated version with a new effective date and provide additional notice when required.

13. Contact

Privacy, deletion, and data-rights requests can be submitted through our Support page. A production contact email must be published there before public launch.
