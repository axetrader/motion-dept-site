Privacy and deletion requests

You may ask Motion Dept to access, correct, or delete personal information associated with your account, including TikTok-sourced data held by Motion Dept, subject to lawful retention requirements.

Send the request from an email address we can use to verify your relationship to the account. Include enough information to identify the connected account, but do not send passwords, access tokens, refresh tokens, API keys, or other credentials.

Disconnecting TikTok

You may revoke Motion Dept's TikTok authorization through TikTok's account/app-permission controls. Motion Dept will also provide a service-side disconnect flow before public launch. Revocation prevents future authorized TikTok API access.
