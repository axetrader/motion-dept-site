Motion Dept is a social media content management and analytics platform for product discovery, content planning, creator-approved publishing, and performance measurement.

What Motion Dept does

Discover

Organizes permitted trend, product, and performance signals so creators can decide what is worth covering.

Create

Helps prepare content ideas, captions, disclosures, and publishing packages while keeping the creator in control.

Measure

Tracks approved publishing outcomes, costs, revenue signals, and content performance using governed analytics.

TikTok integration

Motion Dept is designed to connect to TikTok only after a user authorizes the connection. For the planned web integration, TikTok Login Kit identifies the connected account and the Content Posting API can be used to send creator-approved content to that account.

Motion Dept does not publish merely because content was generated. Before a TikTok post is sent, the creator must be shown the target account, the content, available privacy choices and relevant interaction settings, and must expressly approve the publishing action.

Independent service. Motion Dept is not TikTok and is not endorsed by TikTok. TikTok is a third-party platform with its own terms, policies, permissions, rate limits, and account controls.

Creator control and commercial content

Creators remain responsible for what they publish. Motion Dept is designed to preserve user-selected privacy and interaction settings, respect platform restrictions, and require explicit approval before privileged publishing actions.

When content promotes a business, product, service, affiliate offer, or third party, users are expected to use the disclosures required by TikTok, applicable advertising rules, and any other relevant platform or legal requirements.

Data and security

Credentials and access tokens are intended to be stored on the server side rather than embedded in public code. Motion Dept limits access to authorized scopes and uses provider-specific policy, retention, and approval gates before external actions are enabled.
